# about

I'm an Information Security Analyst/Administrator with a Master's in Cyber Security, a Bachelor's in Information Technology, and CompTIA Security+ (currently studying for CySA+). Day to day, I own the tools and processes that keep an organization's identities, endpoints, and email defensible — from closing audit findings on privileged access to tuning the SIEM rules that catch what matters. I like taking on the parts of security that don't get finished after a single deployment: the ongoing maintenance, the process someone has to actually run, the follow-up that turns a tool into a working control.

# selected work

Continuous Vulnerability Management

Rapid7 InsightVM
situation
The organization relied on ad-hoc penetration tests from a third-party firm, with no continuous vulnerability scanning between engagements — leaving long windows where new vulnerabilities went undetected.
action
Designed and led the full Rapid7 InsightVM deployment: agent rollout across endpoints and servers, scan schedule and policy configuration, and ongoing day-to-day management of the platform.
result
Replaced point-in-time pentests with continuous vulnerability visibility, catching newly introduced weaknesses as they appear rather than waiting for the next scheduled test.

Privileged Access Management Implementation

Keeper Connection Manager
situation
An external compliance audit identified a gap in privileged session management — no centralized PAM solution existed, with staff accessing servers via direct RDP.
action
Took over ownership of the rollout and have since run it end-to-end: configuration, certificate renewal, ongoing maintenance, and vendor liaison with Keeper Security support.
result
~90% of the department now accesses servers through PAM instead of direct RDP, reducing credential exposure and closing the audit's compliance gap.

SIEM Alert Coverage & Tuning

Log360
situation
Insufficient correlation rules and alert conditions limited visibility into anomalous or malicious activity.
action
Built new custom alerts and refined existing correlation rules, adjusting thresholds to close coverage gaps while controlling for false positives.
result
Expanded detection coverage while reducing overall alert noise, improving triage speed and focus for the team.

Security Awareness Program Management

KnowBe4
situation
Needed a reliable way to track and follow up with users who repeatedly failed phishing simulations or lagged on required training.
action
Designed and maintain a noncompliant-user tracking spreadsheet, shared with the helpdesk, so flagged users are followed up with directly and status is updated in real time.
result
The organization's phishing risk score has trended down since the process was introduced.

Email Authentication & Anti-Spoofing

Sophos DMARC
situation
No DMARC policy existed for the organization's email domain, leaving it exposed to spoofing and impersonation attacks.
action
Led the design and rollout of a DMARC policy, deploying it directly to full enforcement rather than a prolonged monitor-only period.
result
Reduced spoofed and impersonation email reaching inboxes, gained visibility into unauthorized senders, and met a compliance requirement.

Automated Privileged Credential Rotation

Keeper Gateway + Vault
situation
Privileged and admin account passwords were changed manually and infrequently, leaving credentials stale for extended periods.
action
Designed and implemented automated rotation for privileged accounts, integrating Keeper Gateway with Keeper Vault.
result
Established a consistent, enforced rotation schedule, significantly cutting the exposure window for stale or compromised credentials.

Least-Privilege AD Delegation

Active Directory · PAM initiative
situation
No formal AD delegation model existed — routine administrative tasks relied on Domain Admin rather than scoped access.
action
Reviewed and advised on the delegation model built by the operations team, flagging risks and gaps to keep it aligned with least-privilege principles and the broader PAM initiative.
result
Reduced reliance on Domain Admin for day-to-day tasks and strengthened alignment with least-privilege access.

Internal HR Site Encryption Migration

Apache · HTTP → HTTPS
situation
The internal HR site ran over plain HTTP, transmitting credentials, compensation data, and personal information in cleartext across the network.
action
Led the migration to HTTPS, implementing a certificate from a public commercial certificate authority.
result
Fully encrypted all traffic to the site, eliminating cleartext exposure of credentials and sensitive employee data.
